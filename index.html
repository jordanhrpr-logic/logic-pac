What Is the Cost of Custom Holiday Gift Set Packaging?

The Challenge

How Do You Produce Influencer Kits That Actually Get Posted?

Influencers receive dozens of PR packages every week. Most get opened, glanced at, and forgotten. The kits that get filmed, photographed, and shared have one thing in common — they were designed with the unboxing moment in mind from the very beginning.

Influencer Kit
Design Philosophy

What Separates a Shareable Influencer Kit from a Forgettable One?

It comes down to three things: the reveal sequence (how each layer unfolds), the tactile experience (how it feels in your hands), and the "camera moment" (the angle that looks best on screen). We engineer all three into every kit we produce.
